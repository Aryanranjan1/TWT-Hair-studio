# **App Name**: TWT Hair Studio Online

## Core Features:

- Service Catalog: Display categorized services with prices, descriptions, and booking CTAs.
- Team Profiles: Showcase stylists with photos, specialties, and Instagram links to build trust.
- Fresha Booking Integration: Embed Fresha widget for appointment scheduling directly on the website.
- SEO Blog: Create and manage blog posts to attract new clients through search engines, and suggest article titles with the help of a tool. A lead capture form encourages conversion of blog readers.
- Gallery: Upload and categorize before/after photos to visually showcase transformations and styles.
- Lead Capture: Collect visitor emails for newsletter and promotional offers via a lead capture form.
- Contact & FAQ: Provide essential information like address, phone number, map, parking details, and answers to common questions, enhancing user experience and reducing inquiries.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft, warm beige (#F5F5DC) to evoke a sense of calm and sophistication, in alignment with salon aesthetics.
- Background color: Light off-white (#FAFAF0), a subtle variation from pure white to add warmth without overpowering the primary color.
- Accent color: Muted gold (#C2B280) to bring a touch of luxury and highlight important elements.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a modern sans-serif with an elegant, fashionable feel; body font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for readability.
- Use minimalist, line-based icons to represent services and categories, keeping a clean and modern aesthetic.
- Implement a clean, spacious layout with a focus on high-quality images and clear calls to action, emphasizing booking and conversion.
- Incorporate subtle animations on button hover and page transitions to enhance interactivity without distracting from the content.
